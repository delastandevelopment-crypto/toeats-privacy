TO Eats Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

TO Eats ("the app", "we", "us") is a Toronto restaurant discovery app published by DelaStan Development. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is stored, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at delastandevelopment@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • TO Eats does not require an account.
  • Your restaurant marks, saved plans, and settings are stored on your device. We do not operate a server that collects them.
  • Location is used only on your device to sort restaurants by distance and to show "near me" results. We do not store your location.
  • The app is free. There are no purchases, subscriptions, or advertising in this version.
  • We do not sell your data.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your phone. This data stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

  • Restaurant marks: which restaurants you have marked as Want to go, Liked, or Visited, and the date you marked a restaurant as visited.
  • Saved plans: the evening plans you choose to save, including their restaurants and times.
  • Photo references: a cache of Google Places photo references, so restaurant photos load faster.
  • Filter and display preferences: your last selected filters and similar settings.

3. Location

If you allow location access, TO Eats uses your device's location to calculate how far restaurants are from you and to show "near me" results. This happens on your device. We do not send your location to our servers and we do not keep a history of where you have been. You can turn off location access at any time in your phone's settings. The app works without it, but distance-based features will not be available.

4. Purchases

TO Eats is free and has no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or advertising. If we add paid features in the future, we will update this policy first and describe exactly what changes.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Places API: restaurant details, opening hours, websites, phone numbers, ratings, and photos come from Google. When the app loads photos or details, Google may receive the request and your IP address. Google's use of this data is described in its privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Directions and websites: when you tap Directions or a restaurant's website, the app opens your device's map app or web browser. Those apps have their own privacy practices.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. The only sharing happens when you choose it: for example, when you export your visits as a file or share a plan, your phone's share sheet sends that content to the app or person you pick.

7. Data we do not collect

TO Eats does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos from your library, or advertising identifiers. The app does not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

8. Children

TO Eats is a general audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The "kid friendly" filter only changes which restaurants are suggested; it does not collect information about children.

9. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete local data: deleting the app removes the data stored on your device.
  • Location: turn off location access in your phone's settings at any time.

10. Security

Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security features. We do not store your personal data on servers, so there is no central database of user information for us to protect. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please keep your device secured with a passcode.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will say so in the app or on this page before it takes effect.

12. Contact

Questions or requests about this policy can be sent to delastandevelopment@gmail.com.

13. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada that apply there.
